# Outfit Randomizer

Build random outfits from your own wardrobe photos. Everything stays on your phone or tablet: no account, no internet needed.

## Download

Go to the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and download the APK for your device:

| File | For |
| --- | --- |
| `OutfitRandomizer-arm64.apk` | Almost every phone and tablet from the last several years. **Try this one first.** |
| `OutfitRandomizer-armv7.apk` | Older 32-bit Android devices. |

You can do this straight from your phone's browser; no computer needed.

## Install

1. Open the downloaded `.apk` file on your Android device.
2. If asked, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps**.
3. If Google Play Protect warns that the app is unrecognised, choose **Install anyway**. The app is shared directly and isn't on the Play Store.
4. Open **Outfit Randomizer**.

Requires **Android 7.0 or newer**.

## What it does

- Generate a random outfit (top, pants, socks, shoes, plus optional hat and watch) and lock the pieces you like.
- Add clothes by photo or from your gallery. The background is removed on the device, and you can crop and rotate.
- Search and organise your wardrobe, and save favourite outfits to a Lookbook.

## Good to know

- Your wardrobe is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
- To update, install the newer APK over the old one. Don't uninstall first.
- iPhone is not supported yet.
